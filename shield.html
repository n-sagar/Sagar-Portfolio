Shield App

Protecting Minors, Empowering Guardians

Mobile App Prototype

Introducing Shield : Protecting Minors, Empowering Guardians In today's connected world, ensuring the online safety of minors is more crucial than ever. Shield is not just an app; it's a commitment to creating a secure digital haven for the guardians and the minors they care for.

Objective : Create an app to help parents/gaurdians protect thier kids online.

Challenge : Many kids today dont understand the issues of talking/sharing info with strangers. Parents have a difficult time regulating or monitoring their kids internet activities. Which is where Shield comes in and alleviate that stress.

Solution : A prototped app to achieve the goals of protecting minors on the internet. With the app we were wanted to provide a simple to use product for the parents/guardians to use.

The Idea

In my UX Fundamentals class, we developed an app prototype called Shield with the aim of helping parents safeguard their children’s online activity. The focus of our project is addressing the issue of minors on the internet and providing parents with control over their children’s online activity. We wanted to bring the safety of monitoring to the guardians. Not everyone is tech savy and with shield they dont need to be! The Idea

The Idea is to provide a solution to Guardians. After gathering our research, we obtained our Target Audience. The majority of users would be any Parent/Gaurdian. Those parents have kids who then classify as Minors. Minors are by law are stated as someone who is under the age of 18 who is under the care of a parent or Gaurdian unless they are emancipated.

Here is some information about Minors and the Target audience. This research was conducted to gain a better understanding of the topic and the perspectives of parents. It also assisted us in designing an app that is both user-friendly and safe!

  • How will we protect minors on the internet?
    - By providing an app that will help monitor users.
  • How will we create a safe environment for the Guardian/Parent and Minor?
    - By allowing users to utilize Shields many safeguarding features, (Content blocking, App/Website blocking, Time Limits and many more.)
  • Who are the Target Audience?
    - Parents/ Guardians anyone who has legal ownership of the child/minor
  • What classifies a Minor?
    - Which is stated by law, someone who is under the age of 18 under the care of a parent or guardian unless they are emancipated.

Personas

These personas were created to help us understand the needs and behaviors of our target audience, which in this case are parents and guardians who want to protect their children online. By understanding their motivations, challenges, and goals, we can design an app that meets their needs effectively. After conducting user research and gathering data, we created two personas: Sarah, a concerned parent, and Oliver, a tech-savvy guardian. These personas helped us empathize with our users and design features that address their specific concerns and needs.

The Design

To achieve this, we designed an app with features such as content blocking, time limits, location tracking, and more. Throughout the design process, we followed Apple’s Human Interface Guidelines to ensure the app is intuitive and user-friendly. My team and I conducted thorough research to showcase the problem and propose our solution.

App Features & Project Focus

Wireframes

I have designed & prototype in the app in Figma. Apple Design principles were used to articulate the design experience. The wireframes showcase the app's user-friendly interface and the various tools available to parents for monitoring and managing their children's online activities.

Main Funtions of Shield

Each wireframe represents a different aspect of the app, highlighting its user-friendly interface and the various tools available to parents for monitoring and managing their children's online activities.

  • Home: displays the user account information.
  • Summary: provides an overview of children's accounts and their online activity.
  • Filters: allows parents to customize content restrictions. By default, teens have preset content blocks that can be adjusted. Additionally, users can enable Safe Search, limit access to social media platforms, and set personalized filters.
  • Map: provides real-time location tracking, allowing parents to check on their child’s whereabouts when needed.

The Outcome

My team and I were delighted to receive an outstanding review and grade for the Shield app prototype. This prototype serves as an eye-opener to the potential dangers that minors face on the internet. In today's world, parents are increasingly seeking ways to safeguard their children, and technology has become an essential tool for achieving this protective solution.

We are proud to have created a prototype that not only addresses these concerns but also provides a user-friendly interface for parents to monitor and manage their children's online activities. The Shield app prototype is a testament to our commitment to enhancing online safety for minors and empowering guardians with the tools they need to protect their loved ones.
